The Assam experience

Nature, culture and unforgettable journeys.

Assam is shaped by the Brahmaputra, lush forests, tea country and communities with rich cultural traditions.

Travel thoughtfully, respect local customs and check current access conditions before visiting protected areas or remote destinations.

  • Tea garden experiences
  • Local food and crafts
  • History and heritage
